## Frontier 0.27.80

- A full browser for agents: a new browser choice in the Preview panel. Frontier opens Chrome or Edge on a profile of its own with agent control switched on, and agents drive every tab and window in it: opening, switching and closing tabs as the task needs, instead of the one tab the Playwright extension gives them. Sign in there once to the sites agents should use; the window stays open between turns and keeps those sign-ins. It is separate from your everyday browser because Chrome refuses agent control of your main profile.
